Give-and-Go Warmup

Beginner 10 min 6+ players

A fast-paced passing warmup that builds first-step quickness and timing.

01

Setup & Instructions

  1. Form two lines facing each other, approximately 15 yards apart. Place a cone 5 yards to the side of one line.
  2. Player 1 throws a forehand to Player 2.
  3. Immediately after releasing the disc, Player 1 sprints diagonally towards the cone.
  4. Player 2 receives the disc and immediately throws a leading backhand pass to Player 1 in stride.
02

Coaching Focus

Explosive Step

Do not watch your throw. Sprint immediately upon release to catch the defense off guard.

Lead Space

Put the disc out in space so the receiver doesn't have to break stride to catch it.
