Give-and-Go Warmup

Beginner 10 min 6+ players

A fast-paced passing warmup that builds first-step quickness and timing. Essential for instilling immediate handler movement after a reset.

01

Setup & Instructions

  1. Form two lines facing each other, approximately 15 yards apart. Place a cone 5 yards to the side of one line to dictate the cut angle.
  2. Player 1 throws a firm forehand to Player 2.
  3. Immediately after releasing the disc, Player 1 sprints diagonally towards the cone without watching the throw.
  4. Player 2 receives the disc and immediately throws a leading backhand pass to Player 1 in stride.
  5. Player 1 catches, pivots, and passes to the next person in line, then jogs to the back of the opposite line.
02

Coaching Focus

Explosive Step

Do not watch your throw. Sprint immediately upon release to catch the defense off guard. The defender should never see your shoulders square up.

Lead Space

Put the disc out in space so the receiver doesn't have to break stride to catch it. Aim for the hip closest to the sideline.

03

Variations

Add a Mark: Place a static mark on the thrower to practice breaking a force before executing the give-and-go cut.
Change Angles: Move the cone directly upfield to force an upline cut instead of a lateral strike.
